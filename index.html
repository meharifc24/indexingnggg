01 — THE MAN

More than strength.

Kyojuro Rengoku is remembered not only for his power, but for the conviction with which he lived.


Cheerful and straightforward, Rengoku possesses a remarkable sense of responsibility. His strength is consistently directed toward protecting others.

Confidence without hesitation.

Rengoku's expression and posture often communicate the same quality: absolute certainty in his purpose.

His confidence is not simply arrogance. It comes from knowing what he believes his duty to be and refusing to abandon it.

The fire surrounding him becomes a reflection of the conviction burning within him.

MUGEN TRAIN

The mission that revealed his character.

Rengoku was assigned to investigate the disappearances aboard the Mugen Train. Tanjiro, Nezuko, Zenitsu and Inosuke would join him on the mission.

The story demonstrates that Rengoku's strength is only part of what makes him remarkable. His responsibility, courage and ability to inspire others become equally important.

His presence leaves something behind: an example for others to carry forward.

06 — THE FLAME

A presence impossible to ignore.

The heroic image captures the visual identity that makes Rengoku immediately recognizable: confidence, intensity and an unmistakable sense of authority.

He does not need exaggerated praise. His presence already speaks for itself.

07 — LEGACY
The flame matters because of what it gives light to.

Rengoku's influence comes from the example he leaves behind. His courage and conviction become something that the people around him can remember and carry forward.
