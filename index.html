01 — Introduction
Kyojuro Rengoku was not defined solely by strength.

He was defined by the principles behind that strength: courage, responsibility, compassion and an unwavering determination to protect those who could not protect themselves.


As the Flame Hashira, he represented the ideals of the Demon Slayer Corps through both his actions and his character. His bright personality concealed neither discipline nor seriousness; instead, the two existed together.

03 — Philosophy

Set your heart ablaze.

Rengoku's philosophy is ultimately about moving forward. Strength carries responsibility, and the purpose of that strength is not personal glory but the protection of others. His optimism is therefore more than personality—it is a deliberate expression of conviction.

04 — Mugen Train
MUGEN TRAIN

A mission involving a rapidly growing number of disappearances aboard the Mugen Train.

The mission that defined his legacy.

Rengoku was sent to investigate the Mugen Train, where more than forty people had disappeared. Tanjiro, Nezuko, Zenitsu and Inosuke would eventually join him aboard the train.

What followed became one of the defining chapters of the series—not simply because of the battle, but because of the example Rengoku set for those who witnessed it.

His resolve never depended on whether victory was guaranteed. His responsibility remained the same regardless of the circumstances.

05 — Legacy
His greatest strength was never simply the power of his sword.

Rengoku's influence comes from the example he leaves behind. His confidence, compassion and determination become lessons carried forward by those around him. He demonstrates that courage is not the absence of fear, but the decision to continue fulfilling one's duty.
